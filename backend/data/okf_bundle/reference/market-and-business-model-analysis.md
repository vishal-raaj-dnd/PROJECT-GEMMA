## 19. Market and Business Model

**Market.** Total addressable market exceeds Rs. 50,000 crore a year (government traffic systems, insurance risk data, emergency integration, fleet APIs). Serviceable by 2027: Rs. 200-450 crore a year across 30-50 procuring cities, 5-10 insurers, 500-1,000 emergency facilities and 50-100 fleet operators. Obtainable by year five: Rs. 150-300 crore across 25-35 cities. The binding constraint is the 6-12 month municipal procurement cycle, not demand.

| Stream | Model | Price | Margin | Strategic role |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Government SaaS | annual subscription: software, updates, cloud, support | Rs. 1-5 L per zone-scale deployment per year, scaling with nodes \[V\] | 65-70% | Establishes the footprint |
| Insurance data | quarterly licence to aggregated risk products | Rs. 2-10 cr per insurer per year | 90% | Monetises footprint at near-zero marginal cost |
| Emergency services | per-facility annual licence | Rs. 5-20 L per facility per year | 75% | Highest retention, faster procurement |
| PPP fine share | service fee on increment in fine revenue | 10% of increment | 95% | Largest by year 4, politically sensitive |
| Fleet and logistics APIs | annual enterprise subscription | Rs. 3-50 L per operator per year | 80% | Defect avoidance, hotspot routing |
| Identity Lane licence | per-authority, home-department procurement | to be priced | to be set | Separate buyer and approval path |

| Year | Gov | Insurance | Emergency | PPP | Fleet | Total (Rs. cr) | Gross margin |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | 0.5 | - | 5 | - | - | 5.5 | 75% |
| 2 | 2 | 10 | 15 | 10 | 5 | 42 | 78% |
| 3 | 9 | 30 | 50 | 40 | 20 | 149 | 80% |
| 4 | 25 | 60 | 80 | 140 | 35 | 340 | 82% |
| 5 | 50 | 100 | 120 | 240 | 50 | 560 | 83% |

The principal structural risk is fine-share revenue: it is the largest stream by years four and five and the most exposed to political reversal.

**Unit economics (50-100 node zone deployment).** Revenue Rs. 50-70 lakh a year; hardware Rs. 5-10 lakh one-time; cloud Rs. 8-12 lakh a year; support Rs. 3-5 lakh a year; gross margin 60-70%; payback 8-12 months. Full-city deployments and the non-municipal streams make the projection table larger than these zone figures, and both scales must be validated in the pilot \[V\]. **Capital plan:** seed Rs. 3-5 crore (months 1-6), Series A Rs. 15-25 crore (month 12), Series B Rs. 50-75 crore (year 2).
