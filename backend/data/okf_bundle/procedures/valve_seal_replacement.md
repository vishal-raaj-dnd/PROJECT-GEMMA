# Hydraulic Valve O-Ring Seal Replacement Procedure

## Disassembly
1. Verify [Hydraulic System Lockout/Tagout (LOTO)](../safety/lockout_tagout_hydraulics.md) has been executed and confirmed zero pressure.
2. Disconnect 24V DC Deutsch plug from valve solenoid.
3. Loosen the 4x M10 hex socket cap screws in an alternating diagonal cross sequence using an 8mm hex bit.
4. Carefully extract the valve body axially from manifold cavity. Do not pry against aluminum sealing surfaces with screwdrivers.

## Cleaning and Inspection
1. Clean the manifold bore using lint-free industrial degreasing wipes.
2. Inspect the internal valve cavity with an inspection mirror for scoring, metal shavings, or contamination.
3. Remove damaged Viton O-ring using a non-marring brass or nylon pick.

## Reassembly and Torquing
1. Lubricate replacement Viton 90-durometer O-ring and PTFE backup ring with clean ISO VG 46 hydraulic oil.
2. Slide new seal over cartridge groove ensuring PTFE backup ring faces the low-pressure side.
3. Seat valve into cavity by hand until flush with manifold face.
4. Using a [Calibrated Digital Torque Wrench](../tools/calibrated_torque_wrench.md), torque the 4x M10 fasteners in three incremental passes: 15 Nm, 25 Nm, and final torque of exactly 35 Nm.
