## 10. Road Intelligence and Urban Analytics

**Defect detection.** Pothole segmentation yields geolocated polygons with severity 1-5 from area and depth cues; repeat observation gives growth rate, the variable that predicts failure. Waterlogging uses texture and reflectance with persistence filtering to separate transient wet road from accumulation. Confirmed defects become work orders carrying location, severity, first-observed date and growth trend, so maintenance is prioritised by risk rather than complaint volume, and affected segments become high-cost edges in the router. Surface defects contribute materially to two-wheeler casualties, so a detection-to-repair cycle of days rather than weeks is a safety intervention.

**Macro analytics.** Hourly volume curves with morning and evening peaks, origin-destination matrices (for example the share of Outer Ring Road entrants bound for Whitefield versus Electronic City), modal split across the 14 vehicle classes (guides bus lanes and EV charging), congestion hotspots with diagnosis of signal timing versus road geometry, cascade root-cause across about 50 junctions, and a three-year pattern store for seasonal adaptation.
