# Electrical Arc Flash & Controller Isolation Protocol

## Isolation Procedure
1. Disconnect upstream 400V 3-phase AC bus feed at the tower base switchgear.
2. Confirm 24V DC auxiliary backup battery bank breaker BB-02 is switched to OFF.
3. Test all exposed terminal blocks with a calibrated CAT IV 1000V multimeter to ensure 0.0V potential.

## Required PPE
- Arc flash face shield (minimum 8 cal/cm2 rating).
- Flame resistant (FR) cotton coveralls.
- Dielectric insulating rubber gloves with leather protectors.
