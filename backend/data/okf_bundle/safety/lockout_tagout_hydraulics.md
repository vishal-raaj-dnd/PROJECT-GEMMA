# Hydraulic System Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) & Zero-Energy Verification

## Isolation Procedure
1. De-energize main hydraulic pump motor breaker (CB-4 in Nacelle Power Distribution Panel).
2. Attach personal safety padlock and OSHA-compliant LOTO tag to manual supply valve HV-01.
3. Open manual bleed needle valve NV-03 slowly into the reservoir return line.
4. Verify residual pressure reads exactly 0.0 bar (0 psi) on analog test gauge PG-02.
5. Discharge nitrogen accumulator bank using the approved 3-way dump valve.

## Required PPE
- Safety glasses with side shields (ANSI Z87.1).
- Heavy-duty nitrile-coated chemical/hydraulic resistant gloves.
- Steel-toe slip-resistant boots.

## Zero-Energy Verification
Technician must physically tap the analog gauge and confirm mechanical zero before unbolting any hydraulic manifold collar.
