# Proportional Pressure Relief Valve V2

## Specifications
- Operating Pressure Range: 15 to 210 bar (217 to 3045 psi).
- Coil Operating Voltage: 24V DC (+/- 10% tolerance), 1.8A max current.
- Port Size: SAE-08 threaded cavity.
- Fastener Torque: 35 Nm (M10 Class 10.9 with light machine oil lubricant).
- Seal Material: Viton 90 Durometer FKM O-ring with PTFE backup ring.

## Visual Identification
- Cylindrical black anodized aluminum enclosure with top-mounted 2-pin IP67 Deutsch electrical connector.
- Stamped laser-etched serial plate on hexagonal collar: `P/N: PRV-992-B`.
- Common failure mode: Radial hydraulic fluid weepage around hex collar caused by extrusion of the internal Viton seal ring under pulse pressure spikes.
