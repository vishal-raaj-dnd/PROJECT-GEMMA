# Calibrated Digital Torque Wrench (10-100 Nm)

## Specifications
- Drive Size: 1/2-inch square drive with quick-release ball detent.
- Operating Range: 10 to 100 Nm (7.4 to 73.8 ft-lbs).
- Accuracy: +/- 2% clockwise between 20% and 100% of full scale.
- Display: Backlit digital LCD showing live peak torque with audible buzzer and LED alert.

## Calibration Standard
- Requires valid ISO/IEC 17025 annual calibration certificate.
- Check calibration label prior to use: do not use if certificate has expired past 12 months.
